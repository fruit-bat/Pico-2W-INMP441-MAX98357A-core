Chirp Real and Complex

A cyclically shifted up-chirp for symbol k plus noise, multiplied by the conjugate base chirp c₀* and transformed with an 1024-point FFT. The real-signal path also folds the in-band bins modulo ⌊BT⌋ to read the symbol off as an angle.

β = k/M mod 1 · α = 1 − β · T = N / fs · B = L·M·fs / N
