Chirp Lab

One received frame through the firmware's decoder: a cyclically shifted real up-chirp for symbol k plus noise is band-passed, de-chirped by C₀*, transformed with an 1024-point FFT, folded coherently into 64 bins, and decided.

B = L·M·fs/N · T = N/fs · 1 symbol = N/M samples = L bins
Decoded k––
Refined position–bins
Timing error (decoder)–samples
True tone offset–samples
Centroid magnitude–0 … 1, tracker needs ≥ 0.8
Strength–(|R|²)^(1/(2+p)); scales with level
Coherent fold gain–at the peak, vs power sum
Decision margin–peak vs next bin (beyond ±1)

1Received signal

cos chirp shifted by k·N/M samples, window offset and time scale applied, plus white noise

2Band-pass

FFT, keep bins – (the chirp band, positive frequencies only), inverse FFT → complex band-limited signal

3De-chirp

× C₀* then FFT. The shifted chirp wraps part way through the frame, so its two pieces land in bins kL and kL − B

4Fold

folded[k] = |X[k] + R·X[k − B]|², R = ej– lines the two pieces up in phase

5Decide

symbol = strongest bin; timing = mean-shift position on the half-bin spectrum, relative to it; centroid gives magnitude and strength
